Before you begin

The webcam is processed on your device. Face reference samples and a rolling video buffer stay in memory. Sustained phone detection, additional faces, face absence, or a changed face may create a video-only evidence clip covering up to 7.5 seconds before and after a flag.

This prototype compares against a reference you capture now. It does not verify your institutional identity or perform liveness checks. Stopping monitoring deletes buffered frames and reference samples; clips already submitted remain subject to the stated retention policy.

For real exams, your institution must supply its approved privacy notice and alternative assessment process.
